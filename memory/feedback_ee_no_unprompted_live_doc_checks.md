Don't re-check the live Extended Essay document (via SharePoint download + extraction, see [[ee-extended-essay-handoff]]) after Matteo reports he's made an edit or picked an option. Only re-verify against the live doc when he explicitly says to check it.

**Why:** Told directly during a 2026-09-03 session, after the assistant tried to re-download and re-extract the doc to confirm a combined-options edit Matteo said he'd already made, before moving to the next item. The confirm-before-trusting instinct (Section 2.2 of the EE handoff, about checking assembled sentences for compounding damage) applies to sentences the assistant itself assembles from chosen options, not to edits Matteo says he already made himself in the live doc.

**How to apply:** Take Matteo's word for what's in the document (e.g. "I went with a combination", "done", "fixed") and move on to the next item without a live-doc check. Only re-extract/re-check when he asks directly (e.g. "check the live doc", "verify this"). This does not override checking a *newly assembled sentence* he hasn't typed himself for compounding issues — that's still per the handoff's own methodology.
