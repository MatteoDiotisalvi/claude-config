When reviewing a paragraph of the Extended Essay, describe what is wrong with each part (quote the phrase, state the issue). Then:
- If the fix is small/mechanical (a word swap, a consistency fix, a short clause), suggest 2 alternative phrasings for Matteo to pick between or adapt.
- If the fix is larger (a sentence needs restructuring, a claim needs rethinking, multiple issues compound in one sentence, or it touches argument structure), just describe the problem and stop — do not phrase it. Matteo writes the actual correction himself.

**Why:** established 2026-09-02 while working through Paragraph 3 corrections. Matteo wants to do the substantive rewriting himself but finds small binary choices (e.g. "systematic trading" vs "systematic equity trading" placement) useful to have pre-drafted, since they don't require his own judgement the way a restructured sentence does.

**How to apply:** this refines the general rule in [[project_extended_essay]] ("give concrete feedback, don't write the corrected sentence unless asked") — it's not an exception to that rule, it's the specific threshold for when a small pre-drafted option counts as "concrete feedback" rather than "doing the rewrite for him."
