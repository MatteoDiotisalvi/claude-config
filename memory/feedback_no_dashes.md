Never use em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) when writing for Matteo. He does not use them naturally and actively removes them when he sees them.

**Why:** Matteo confirmed he noticed em dashes in two portfolio entries and manually removed them after posting. His rule: dashes only appear in his writing when a spell-checker or grammar checker (e.g. Word) flags that one is required — never as a stylistic choice.

**How to apply:** Replace all em/en dashes with parentheses for asides, commas for brief interruptions, or a period to split the sentence. This applies to all writing deliverables (portfolio entries, essays, emails, personal statements). The write skill already notes this tendency but Matteo has now confirmed it as a firm rule, not just a preference.

It also covers project documents Claude writes (plans, research memos, markdown files) and chat replies. On 2026-09-15 this slipped badly during the long trading research session: 124 dashes ended up across 8 files in `~\trading` and had to be bulk-replaced. Long, fast-written documents are where it creeps back in, so before finishing any multi-file writing task, grep the output for "—" and "–" and fix them by rewriting the sentence, not by a blind find-and-replace (a mechanical swap left a couple of broken sentences that needed manual repair).
