When Matteo pastes in a report from another AI tool or research assistant (recognizable by garbled citation artifacts like "youtube+5" mixed into the prose, or just by being handed over wholesale as "here's another research report"), treat every specific claim in it as an unverified lead to independently check, not as established fact, even when it's well-formatted and reads confidently.

**Why:** validated twice in [[project-applications]]. First, a report recommending "Coconut Context" as a connector for centralizing his application data turned out to be an unverified, community-tier third-party MCP server from a small unvetted company, recommending it uncritically would have routed his personal data through an unaccountable third party. Second, a pasted table of "virtual mentors" contained two names that didn't hold up on independent research: "Mnemonic Education" had no actual Imperial-specific content anywhere despite the claim, and "MARYADI – Ray" couldn't be tied to any real person despite the company itself being real, matching the shape of a fabricated or badly mischaracterized entry. Both would have gone into his project files as trustworthy if taken at face value.

**How to apply:** when a pasted report names a specific tool, person, company, or claim, verify it against something other than the report's own text (the actual product/company's own site, LinkedIn, independent press, a university's own page) before adding it to any of his project files. If verification fails, exclude it and say plainly why, rather than passing it through. This isn't specific to the UCAS project, apply it to any pasted external research regardless of topic.
