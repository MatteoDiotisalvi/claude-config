When writing IB English A Learner Portfolio entries, use a register that is slightly less formal than graded academic essays. The portfolio is not assessed, so Matteo would not write as strictly as in a Paper 1 or essay.

**Why:** Matteo noticed the first entries read a bit too academically stiff. He gave "merely" -> "only" as an example of the kind of swap needed. The goal is still clear and analytical, but it should sound like a real person reflecting, not a formal essay.

**How to apply:** Prefer plain everyday words over formal/Latinate ones where they mean the same thing. E.g.: "merely" -> "only", "utilised" -> "used", "subsequently" -> "then", "analysed" -> "looked at" or "went through". Keep the analytical thinking; just relax the vocabulary register slightly. No slang, no casual filler — just less stiff.
