A compiled reference of high-scoring Computer Science EE exemplars and official/authoritative IB guidance lives at `OneDrive\School\Extended essay\EE_CS_Analysis_Reference.md`. Check it during EE drafting/review work rather than re-researching from scratch.

Key pattern to apply: every A-grade exemplar found named the *specific* methods/models being compared and the *specific* metrics used directly in the RQ (e.g. "Bidirectional LSTM vs LinearSVC... in terms of classification accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 Score"); every B/C-grade exemplar used generic terms like "AI" or "machine learning models" instead. The single most repeated criticism across official IB guidance, a real CS EE examiner subject report, and third-party guides alike was essays being "overly descriptive" rather than analytical/evaluative.

Also confirmed via official IB CS subject-specific guidance: an essay making social/economic implications "a major focus" belongs in ITGS, not CS — directly relevant to [[project_extended_essay]]'s conversion away from the essay's economics content.

Open flag noted in the reference doc: two different IB EE assessment models are circulating (old 34-point vs new 30-point, first assessed May 2027) with different criteria weightings — confirmed with Matteo as the new model applies to him.
