When Matteo says a section of the Extended Essay is "unfinished" and asks to fix/complete it, don't jump straight to a full drafted rewrite. Describe the issues first (what's missing, what's weak, why), then wait for his input, then draft additions collaboratively — even though he authorized moving forward ("go ahead").

**Why:** Told directly during a 2026-09-03 session, after the assistant was asked to finish the Conclusion and responded with a complete two-paragraph rewrite (fixed sentence + new limitations/future-research content) in one shot. Matteo's feedback: "this is done too fast and without my input." "Go ahead" authorized proceeding with the task, not skipping the describe-first step that the rest of the session's methodology (see [[ee-extended-essay-handoff]] Section 2.1) already establishes for larger/structural issues — a full paragraph of new argumentative content is the largest category of "larger issue" there is, so it needs the diagnosis step even more than a single sentence would, not less.

**How to apply:** For any "this needs more work" / "finish this" type request on the EE, first state what's missing or weak, in list or prose form, without drafting replacement text. Let Matteo react, redirect, or ask for a draft before writing one. This applies even under explicit authorization to proceed — "go ahead" means keep working on the task, not skip a step of the established collaborative process.
